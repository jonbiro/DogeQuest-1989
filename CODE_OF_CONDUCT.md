# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Puppy Quest 1989 to be a welcoming project for contributors of every background and experience level. Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- welcome different viewpoints and accessibility needs; and
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and other unwelcome conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, chat, and project-related events, as well as conduct that affects a participant's safety in those spaces.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. They will handle reports as privately as practical and will explain moderation decisions when doing so is safe and appropriate.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/jonbiro). Do not post sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
