Rewards, routes and special moves

Points and upgrades
Ten bones in a row earn +100 points. Clean jumps and slides earn +20. Finish the run to bank your score and any completed challenge reward for Paw upgrades. Each run offers up to three challenges in order. Progress from the whole run counts; completed goals reveal the next one. All earned challenge bonuses are banked together when the run ends. Need a break? Pause, then choose “Finish & bank points” to keep what you earned without losing your remaining hearts. Every five clean jumps, slides, course weaves or marked turns earns a clean-move bonus: +50, +100, +150, then +200 at each milestone. A collision or shield save breaks this streak; missing a bone does not. Automatic Zoomies obstacle clears do not count.

Obstacles and pickups
Jump logs and low stone blocks. Slide under arches, branches, and hanging gates. Purple gems give 250 points; gold tokens double bone points for 10 seconds; hearts heal, or earn 100 points at full health. Shields block one hit; collecting a spare shield earns 100 points. Gift boxes give 100 points. Finish your run to bank them toward clubhouse prizes. Spend points on puppies and outfits at camp. Tennis balls trigger six seconds of Zoomies: run faster and smash obstacles for 40 points each. Watch the timer—then jump and slide again.

Regions and marked turns
Every 450 meters, discover the next region: jungle, canyon, then crystal glade. At lime chevron signs, swipe left or right when the matching turn arrow appears. One swipe locks in the corner and earns 100 points. Missing it costs a heart; the trail recovers safely. Between marked corners, left and right switch lanes. The trail also climbs and descends; jump timing stays the same. Jump broken trail sections with striped edges. Missing a jump costs a heart; a shield can rescue you. Zoomies jumps gaps automatically.

Choose your route
At trail gates, take the green left gate for fewer obstacles or the gold right gate for tougher patterns and 60-point clean clears. Staying in the center selects Scenic. Each choice lasts 220 meters. Scenic takes a gentler left-hand detour; Challenge sweeps right. Both rejoin the main trail. These bends are automatic—only marked corners need a turn swipe. Outside Scenic sections, watch for regional courses: jump and duck through jungle roots, jump canyon gaps, and weave through glade crystals. Follow all three beats for 180 extra points and regional mastery credit. Finish two of three cleanly for a 60-point recovery bonus instead; partial courses do not earn regional mastery. A gift and a clear recovery stretch follow the final obstacle.

Trail passport
Your trail passport keeps dog bonds and regional stamps across runs. Clears, clean course weaves and turns build your chosen dog's bond; clean regional courses earn stamps. Finish a run to bank progress and milestone rewards, then open the passport in the clubhouse to see your next goal.

Fetch: your earned magnet
Fetch is your earned power: collect bones by hand (+2% each), clear obstacles or weave beats in named courses (+12%), and take marked turns (+20%). At 100%, tap FETCH or press F for a four-second magnet burst. Save it for a rich bone trail! It cannot recharge itself or replace an active magnet.

Floating bones: catch the zipline
High floating bones are on the zipline, not within normal jump reach. Jump when the turquoise handle reaches Mochi to catch the cable, then follow the small BONES/GIFT arrows to steer between lanes. Bone arrows stay quiet during a magnet; gifts still need steering. Finishing earns 250 points. Jump and slide are suspended until the automatic landing. Missed the handle? Keep running safely on the trail underneath.

Mixed obstacles and protection
Bones may lead into obstacles. Full-width logs require a jump; later split rows put logs beside an overhead gate: choose a lane, then jump its log or slide under its gate. Scenic skips split rows. Full-width gates require a slide. Swipe at marked turns to take the corner; elsewhere, swipe to change lanes. Magnets collect nearby ground bones (or aerial bones while riding); shields absorb one hit.
