How river crossings work

The first river begins at 1,150 meters on new trails. Your puppy boards the raft automatically. Follow the bone lanes with left/right swipes, buttons or enabled tilt. Steer early: the raft carries momentum.

River rocks cannot be jumped or slid under. Those moves return at the shore. Magnets and Fetch still collect nearby bones; steer to collect the gift. Reach the far shore for +250 score points.

Completed rides build River explorer passport stamps when you bank the run. Practice is unscored and never advances the passport. Try River steering in the practice trails above. Older shared trails keep their original layouts without rivers.

Scenic or Challenge?

← Scenic · explore at your pace
Take the green left gate, or stay in the center. New trails include gentle regional encounters with two open lanes per obstacle. Follow the bones, steer around hazards, or jump and slide for normal clear points. These optional encounters do not earn regional stamps.

Challenge → · chase clean-course rewards
Take the gold right gate for tougher patterns and 60-point clears. Regional courses also appear outside Scenic: follow all three beats for +180 points and mastery credit. Two clean beats earn +60 instead, without mastery. A gift and recovery stretch follow each course.

Each gate choice lasts 220 meters. Scenic bends left and Challenge bends right, then both rejoin. These bends are automatic; only marked corners need a turn swipe. Older shared trails retain their original Scenic layouts.

Rewards and special moves

Points and upgrades
Ten bones in a row earn +100 points. Clean jumps and slides earn +20. Finish the run to bank your score and any completed challenge reward for Paw upgrades. Each run offers up to three challenges in order. Progress from the whole run counts; completed goals reveal the next one. All earned challenge bonuses are banked together when the run ends. Need a break? Pause, then choose “Finish & bank points” to keep what you earned without losing your remaining hearts. Every five clean jumps, slides, course weaves or marked turns earns a clean-move bonus: +50, +100, +150, then +200 at each milestone. A collision or shield save breaks this streak; missing a bone does not. Automatic Zoomies obstacle clears do not count.

Obstacles and pickups
Jump logs and low stone blocks. Slide under arches, branches, and hanging gates. Purple gems give 250 points; gold tokens double bone points for 10 seconds; hearts heal, or earn 100 points at full health. Shields block one hit; collecting a spare shield earns 100 points. Gift boxes give 100 points. Area relics appear at the end of authored courses, match the landscape colors and give 160 points when collected. Finish your run to bank gifts and keep the relic points in your score. Spend points on puppies and outfits at camp. Tennis balls trigger six seconds of Zoomies: run faster and smash obstacles for 40 points each. Watch the timer—then jump and slide again.

Regions and marked turns
Explore six areas: Sunleaf Woods, Bamboo Sanctuary, Redrock Pass, Palm Oasis, Crystal Reach and Mooncap Grove. Every 450 meters, discover the next mastery region: jungle, canyon, then crystal glade. At lime chevron signs, swipe left or right when the matching turn arrow appears. One swipe locks in the corner and earns 100 points. Missing it costs a heart; the trail recovers safely. Between marked corners, left and right switch lanes. The trail also climbs and descends; jump timing stays the same. Jump broken trail sections with striped edges. Missing a jump costs a heart; a shield can rescue you. Zoomies jumps gaps automatically.

Trail passport
Your trail passport keeps dog bonds, regional stamps and completed river and cable rides across runs. Clears, clean course weaves and turns build your chosen dog's bond; clean regional courses earn stamps. Finish a run to bank progress and milestone rewards, then open the passport in the clubhouse to see your next goal.

Fetch: your earned magnet
Fetch is your earned power: collect bones by hand (+2% each), clear obstacles or weave beats in named courses (+12%), and take marked turns (+20%). At 100%, tap FETCH or press F for a four-second magnet burst. Save it for a rich bone trail! It cannot recharge itself or replace an active magnet.

Floating bones: catch the zipline
High floating bones are on the zipline, not within normal jump reach. Jump when the turquoise handle reaches Mochi to catch the cable, then follow the small BONES/GIFT arrows to steer between lanes. Bone arrows stay quiet during a magnet; gifts still need steering. Finishing earns 250 points. Jump and slide are suspended until the automatic landing. Missed the handle? Keep running safely on the trail underneath.

Mixed obstacles and protection
Bones may lead into obstacles. Full-width logs require a jump; later split rows put logs beside an overhead gate: choose a lane, then jump its log or slide under its gate. Scenic skips split rows. Full-width gates require a slide. Swipe at marked turns to take the corner; elsewhere, swipe to change lanes. Magnets collect nearby ground bones (or aerial bones while riding); shields absorb one hit.
