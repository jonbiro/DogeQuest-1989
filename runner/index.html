Run rewards and details

Invite someone to the same starting trail, with an optional score to beat. Targets are friendly and unverified, not ranked. Players use their own upgrades; route choices can change later obstacles. Links preserve the trail-generation version.

Touch & drag controls

The big movement buttons are the simplest option: tap LEFT or RIGHT for one lane, JUMP to clear a log or gap, and SLIDE under an overhead gate. For swipes, press and hold on the trail, then make one short drag for one move. To keep your finger down, stop your thumb briefly, then drag again; lifting is always okay. A clear swipe in another direction can follow a jump or slide without lifting. You can also tap a left or right edge to steer, or the center to jump. The movement buttons stay available by default; check this to hide them for a cleaner trail. Keyboard controls still work.

Sound atmosphere

Quiet phrases give each landscape its own sound. Turn sound on with ♫ to hear them, or switch this off for gameplay effects only. Practice stays quiet apart from action feedback.

Your current challenge

Today's shared trail

A fresh starting layout every day at midnight UTC. Everyone gets the same seed and uses their own upgrades; route choices can change later obstacles. No rankings, streak penalties or special currency. Select it, run, then share your score from the results. Older links remain replayable.

Race your local ghost

Finish a seeded trail once and Puppy Run keeps a small replay on this device. Retry the same trail to see a mint ghost pacing 24 meters ahead; match its lanes and action timing to chase your best. Ghosts never collide, change scores or leave this browser.

Weekly fetch

A fresh local goal rotates every Monday at midnight UTC. Complete adventures to build progress across the week, then finish the target to earn the points shown on the menu. It is saved only in this browser, never ranked, and practice runs do not count.

Adventure streaks

Finish one scored adventure on consecutive UTC days to keep your local streak alive. Missed days simply reset it to one on your next run—there are no penalties. Three, seven, fourteen and thirty-day milestones award the points shown in your results. Everything stays in this browser; practice runs do not count.

Trail perks

Pick one free perk at camp before an adventure. Treat pouch blocks your first collision, Scent burst pulls nearby bones for eight seconds, and Calm start forgives collisions for four seconds. Perks expire during the run and never change the points earned for ordinary moves.

Try the practice trails

Choose a skill. Jump and slide drills build speed as you improve. No penalties; your upgrades apply.

How river crossings work

The first river begins at 1,150 meters on new trails. Your puppy boards the raft automatically. Follow the bone lanes with left/right drag left/right or buttons. Steer early: the raft carries momentum.

River rocks cannot be jumped or slid under. Those moves return at the shore. Magnets and Fetch still collect nearby bones; steer to collect the gift. Reach the far shore for +250 score points.

Completed rides build River explorer passport stamps when you bank the run. Practice is unscored and never advances the passport. Try River steering in the practice trails above. Older shared trails keep their original layouts without rivers.

How mine-cart rides work

The first mine-cart section begins at 7,200 meters on new trails. Your puppy boards automatically; keep tapping or dragging LEFT and RIGHT to follow the glowing open lane between the rocks.

Scenic carts give you a choice: stay with the steady bone lane, or chase the glowing gem lane for +250 points each. Challenge carts keep one open lane so the timing stays fair.

Mine-cart rocks cannot be jumped or slid under. Jump and slide return after the cart exits. Reach the green exit marker for a +250 completion bonus.

Scenic or Challenge?

← Scenic · explore at your pace
Take the green left gate, or stay in the center. New trails include gentle regional encounters with two open lanes per obstacle. Follow the bones, steer around hazards, or jump and slide for normal clear points. These optional encounters do not earn regional stamps.

Challenge → · chase clean-course rewards
Take the right Challenge gate for tougher patterns and 60-point clears. Regional courses also appear outside Scenic: follow all three beats for +180 points and mastery credit. Two clean beats earn +60 instead, without mastery. A gift and recovery stretch follow each course.

Each gate choice lasts 220 meters. Scenic bends left and Challenge bends right, then both rejoin. These bends are automatic; only marked corners need a turn swipe. Older shared trails retain their original Scenic layouts.

Rewards and special moves

Points and upgrades
Ten bones in a row earn +100 points. Clean jumps and slides earn +20. Finish the run to bank your score and any completed challenge reward for Paw upgrades. Each run offers up to three challenges in order. Progress from the whole run counts; completed goals reveal the next one. All earned challenge bonuses are banked together when the run ends. Need a break? Pause, then choose “Finish & bank points” to keep what you earned without losing your remaining hearts. Every five clean jumps, slides, course weaves or marked turns earns a clean-move bonus: +50, +100, +150, then +200 at each milestone. A collision or shield save breaks this streak; missing a bone does not. Automatic Zoomies obstacle clears do not count. The compact flow meter in the run HUD shows how close you are to the next bonus.

Obstacles and pickups
Jump logs and low stone blocks. Slide under arches, branches, and hanging gates. Purple gems give 250 points; bone doublers give 2× bone points for 10 seconds; hearts heal, or earn 100 points at full health. Shields block one hit; collecting a spare shield earns 100 points. Gift boxes give 100 points. Area relics appear at the end of authored courses, match the landscape colors and give 160 points when collected. Finish your run to bank gifts and keep the relic points in your score. Spend points on puppies and outfits at camp. Tennis balls trigger six seconds of Zoomies: run faster and smash obstacles for 40 points each. Watch the timer—then jump and slide again. Mine-cart rides board automatically, reward +250 when completed, and ask you to steer between rocks while jump and slide are unavailable. Scenic carts offer a steady bone lane or a risky gem lane worth +250 per gem; challenge carts keep one open lane.

Regions and marked turns
Explore six areas: Sunleaf Woods, Bamboo Sanctuary, Redrock Pass, Palm Oasis, Crystal Reach and Mooncap Grove. Every 450 meters, discover the next mastery region: jungle, canyon, then crystal glade. At lime chevron signs, swipe left or right when the matching turn arrow appears. One swipe locks in the corner and earns 100 points. Missing it costs a heart; the trail recovers safely. Between marked corners, left and right switch lanes. The trail also climbs and descends; jump timing stays the same. Jump broken trail sections with striped edges. Missing a jump costs a heart; a shield can rescue you. Zoomies jumps gaps automatically.

Each destination has its own mechanic: follow the Fern weave around roots in Sunleaf, read the Lantern slalom in Bamboo, jump the Ridge hops in Redrock, choose the next Stepping stone in the Oasis, follow Prism timing in Crystal Reach, and use the quiet gap in Mooncap's Moonlit weave. Landmarks, props, lighting and pickup patterns change with the landscape, while the move cues stay familiar.

Trail passport
Your trail passport keeps dog bonds, regional stamps and completed river and cable rides across runs. Clears, clean course weaves and turns build your chosen dog's bond; clean regional courses earn stamps. Finish a run to bank progress and milestone rewards, then open the passport in the clubhouse to see your next goal.

Fetch: your earned magnet
Fetch is your earned power: collect bones by hand (+2% each), clear obstacles or weave beats in named courses (+12%), and take marked turns (+20%). At 100%, tap FETCH or press F for a four-second magnet burst. Save it for a rich bone trail! It cannot recharge itself or replace an active magnet.

Floating bones: catch the zipline
High floating bones are on the zipline, not within normal jump reach. Jump when the turquoise handle reaches Mochi to catch the cable, then follow the small BONES/GIFT arrows to steer between lanes. Bone arrows stay quiet during a magnet; gifts still need steering. Finishing earns 250 points. Jump and slide are suspended until the automatic landing. Missed the handle? Keep running safely on the trail underneath.

Mixed obstacles and protection
Bones may lead into obstacles. Full-width logs require a jump; later split rows put logs beside an overhead gate: choose a lane, then jump its log or slide under its gate. Scenic skips split rows. Full-width gates require a slide. Swipe at marked turns to take the corner; elsewhere, drag to change lanes. Magnets collect nearby ground bones (or aerial bones while riding); shields absorb one hit.
