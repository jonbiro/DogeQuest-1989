Ten bones in a row earn +100 points. Clean jumps and slides earn +20. Finish the run to bank your score and any completed challenge reward for Paw upgrades.

Jump logs and low stone blocks. Slide under arches, branches, and hanging gates. Purple gems give 250 points; gold tokens double bone points for 10 seconds; hearts heal. Gift boxes give 100 points. Finish your run to bank them toward clubhouse prizes. Spend points on puppies and outfits at camp. Tennis balls trigger six seconds of Zoomies: run faster and smash obstacles for 40 points each. Watch the timer—then jump and slide again. Every 450 meters, discover the next region: jungle, canyon, then crystal glade. At lime chevron signs, swipe left or right when the matching turn arrow appears. One swipe locks in the corner and earns 100 points. Missing it costs a heart; the trail recovers safely. Between marked corners, left and right switch lanes. The trail also climbs and descends; jump timing stays the same. Jump broken trail sections with striped edges. Missing a jump costs a heart; a shield can rescue you. Zoomies jumps gaps automatically. At trail gates, take the green left gate for fewer obstacles or the gold right gate for tougher patterns and 60-point clean clears. Staying in the center selects Scenic. Each choice lasts 220 meters. Outside Scenic sections, watch for regional courses: jump and duck through jungle roots, jump canyon gaps, and weave through glade crystals. Follow all three beats for 180 extra points. A gift and a clear recovery stretch follow the final obstacle.

Your trail passport keeps dog bonds and regional stamps across runs. Clean clears and turns build your chosen dog's bond; clean regional courses earn stamps. Finish a run to bank progress and milestone rewards, then open the passport in the clubhouse to see your next goal.

Fetch is your earned power: collect bones by hand (+2% each), clear obstacles (+12%), and take marked turns (+20%). At 100%, tap FETCH or press F for a four-second magnet burst. Save it for a rich bone trail! It cannot recharge itself or replace an active magnet.

High floating bones are on the zipline, not within normal jump reach. Jump when the turquoise handle reaches Mochi to catch the cable, then follow the small BONES/GIFT arrows to steer between lanes. Bone arrows stay quiet during a magnet; gifts still need steering. Finishing earns 250 points. Jump and slide are suspended until the automatic landing. Missed the handle? Keep running safely on the trail underneath.
